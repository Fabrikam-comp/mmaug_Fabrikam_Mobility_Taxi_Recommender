How to request a ride

Get from A to B in just a few taps. It's simple.

1 Get started

Enter your pickup and dropoff locations in the app. Choose a ride and confirm.

2 Get matched with a driver

A nearby driver accepts your request. You'll be notified when they're close.

3 Meet your driver

Verify your driver's name, vehicle details and your destination, then enter the vehicle.

4 Enjoy the ride

Your driver has turn-by-turn directions for the fastest route.

5 Rate and tip

Please leave feedback about your trip, and you'll also be able to tip your driver.
